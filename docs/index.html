Frequently asked questions

Some handwriting was not erased.

On the review screen, raise the Sensitivity, or use the Mark to erase brush to paint over the strokes that were missed, then tap Erase handwriting again. Pen ink that is the same colour as the print is detected best with the Ink colour + AI method.

Some printed text was removed.

Use the Keep brush on the review screen to protect that text before erasing. The original capture is always kept, so you can open the document again from Documents and tap Edit again.

Where are my documents stored?

On your device, inside the app. Every scan keeps its untouched original; the processed version is saved next to it when you tap Save to documents. Deleting the app deletes them, so export anything you want to keep.

Which formats can I export?

PDF (one A4 page per scan), Word (.docx) and PNG images. Tap Share to send them to any app, or Save images to put them in your photo library.

How do I change the app language?

Tap the language button at the top right of the Scan tab. English, Vietnamese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and French are available.
